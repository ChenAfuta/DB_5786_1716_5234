Billing & Finance Division
1. Invoice Management Screen

Purpose:
This screen is designed to allow billing staff to manage invoices within the hospital system. It provides a centralized view of all invoices and enables efficient tracking and management of billing records.

Displayed Data:

invoice_id
patient_id
admission_id
invoice_date
total_amount

User Actions:

Create a new invoice
Edit an existing invoice
Search for invoices by ID, patient, or date
View detailed invoice information

Related Database Tables:

Invoice

Explanation:
Each row in the table represents a single invoice. The displayed attributes correspond directly to the fields defined in the Invoice table in the database schema.

2. Invoice Details Screen

Purpose:
This screen displays detailed information about a selected invoice, including all services and charges associated with it.

Displayed Data:

invoice_id
patient_id
admission_id
invoice_date
total_amount

Invoice Items Section:

item_id
invoice_id
service_name
cost

User Actions:

Add a new invoice item
Edit an existing item
Delete an item
View the total cost of the invoice

Related Database Tables:

Invoice
Invoice_Item

Explanation:
This screen represents the one-to-many relationship between Invoice and Invoice_Item, where a single invoice may contain multiple service entries.

3. Payments and Refunds Screen

Purpose:
This screen is responsible for managing all financial transactions related to invoices, including payments and refunds.

Displayed Data – Payments:

payment_id
invoice_id
payment_date
amount
payment_method

Displayed Data – Refunds:

refund_id
payment_id
refund_date
amount

User Actions:

Record a new payment
View payment history
Issue a refund for a payment
View refund history

Related Database Tables:

Payment
Refund

Explanation:
This screen reflects the relationship between payments and refunds. Each payment may be associated with a refund, depending on the business logic of the system.

4. Insurance Claims and Billing Staff Screen

Purpose:
This screen manages insurance claim processes and billing staff assignments within the billing system.

Displayed Data – Insurance Claims:

claim_id
invoice_id
insurance_id
claim_status

Displayed Data – Billing Staff:

billing_staff_id
staff_id
role

User Actions:

Create a new insurance claim
Update claim status
Assign billing staff to tasks
Manage staff roles

Related Database Tables:

Insurance_Claim
Billing_Staff

Explanation:
This screen integrates two related aspects of billing operations: insurance claim handling and staff management. It ensures that each claim is tracked and that responsibilities are assigned to appropriate staff members.
